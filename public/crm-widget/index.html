Document Generator
Upload a template, place fields, and save the finished document to this record
How it works
  1. New template. Upload your document design (PNG / JPG / PDF) or start from a blank page.
  2. Place fields. Click on the design to drop a field, then pick which CRM field it maps to (the list is pulled from this module). Add a logo too if you like.
  3. Save & use. Save the template (kept for next time). Then Pull from record to fill it, and Save to record to attach the finished document.
