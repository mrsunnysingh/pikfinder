A good business card still does a lot of work — it's the thing people keep after a meeting. You can design one that looks professionally made in just a few minutes, for free.

## What to put on a business card

Keep it clean and easy to scan:

- Your name and title
- Company name and logo
- Phone and email
- Website or social handle

Leave breathing room — a cramped card looks cheap. One accent colour and one clear font go a long way.

## Design one in three steps

1. **Start from a template.** Open the free [business card templates](/templates/free-business-card-templates) and pick a premium layout.
2. **Make it yours.** Edit the text, swap in your brand colour, and add your logo.
3. **Download.** Export a print-ready PDF or a high-resolution PNG — no signup, no watermark.

## Print tips that avoid surprises

- **Standard size** is 3.5 × 2 inches (or 85 × 55 mm). The templates are already set up for print.
- **Add bleed** if your printer asks for it, so colours run to the edge.
- **Use high resolution** — export at the highest quality so text stays crisp.
- **Order a small batch first** to check colours before printing hundreds.
