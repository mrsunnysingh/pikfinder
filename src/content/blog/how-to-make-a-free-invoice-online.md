Sending a clean, professional invoice makes you look organised and helps you get paid faster. You don't need accounting software or a designer — you can make one online for free in a couple of minutes.

## What a good invoice needs

Every invoice should include a few essentials so it's clear and easy to pay:

- Your business name and contact details
- The client's name and details
- A unique invoice number and the date
- A short description of the work or products
- The amount due, and how to pay
- Payment terms (e.g. "due within 14 days")

## Make one in three steps

1. **Pick a template.** Open the free [invoice templates](/templates/free-invoice-templates) and choose a layout that matches your brand.
2. **Add your details.** Edit the business name, client, invoice number, date and amount right in the browser. Drop in your logo for a polished look.
3. **Download.** Export a print-ready PDF or a high-resolution PNG — no signup, no watermark.

## Send the same invoice to many clients at once

If you bill lots of customers, you don't have to redo the layout every time. In the [Document Generator](/business-automation) you can design an invoice once and auto-fill a personalised copy for every customer from a spreadsheet or your Zoho CRM — one invoice or a thousand, in a single click.

## Tips to get paid faster

- Put the amount due and due date near the top where they're easy to see.
- Add clear payment instructions (UPI, bank transfer, card link).
- Number your invoices sequentially so they're easy to track.
- Send a friendly reminder a few days before the due date.
