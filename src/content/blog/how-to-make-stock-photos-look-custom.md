Free stock photo platforms like Unsplash, Pexels, and Pixabay offer stunning high-resolution photography. The challenge? Millions of creators are using the exact same popular photos.

When potential clients see the same smiling model or office handshake photo on your landing page that they saw on three other websites this morning, brand trust drops.

The good news: with just 2 to 3 minutes of editing, you can transform any stock photo into an asset that looks completely customized for your brand. Here is how.

## 1. Crop for a Unconventional Point of View

Most photographers shoot wide angles to make their photos versatile. The default center framing is what makes a stock photo instantly recognizable.

Instead of using the full image:
- Zoom into a specific detail (hands holding a mug, laptop keyboard reflections, textures).
- Shift the subject off-center using the rule of thirds to leave ample negative space for your headline.
- Crop into tight portrait or vertical frames for social slides.

## 2. Apply a Subtle Brand Color Tint

One of the easiest ways to unify imagery across your website is color grading.

- Add a semi-transparent layer in your brand's primary color (at 10% to 20% opacity) with a blend mode like **Overlay**, **Multiply**, or **Soft Light**.
- This casts a harmonious tone across shadows and highlights, making disparate photos from different photographers feel like they belong to the same campaign photoshoot.

## 3. Use Duotone & Gradient Maps for Tech and Modern Brands

If your brand identity has vibrant secondary colors (like purple, electric blue, or neon lime), turn your photo into a duotone:

1. Desaturate the photo into high-contrast black and white.
2. Map your dark brand color to the shadows and your light brand color to the highlights.
3. The result is a sleek, modern visual asset that looks 100% bespoke.

## 4. Remove Distracting Backgrounds

Sometimes the subject of the photo (a person, gadget, or product) is perfect, but the cluttered background looks like a generic corporate office.

Use our instant [Background Remover Tool](/tools/remove-background) to cut out the subject, then place them on:
- A clean solid color from your brand palette.
- A subtle abstract gradient canvas.
- A textured minimalist pattern.

## 5. Add Brand Graphic Accents & Floating Badges

Don't just place a photo alone in a container. Integrate it with custom UI accents:
- Place subtle vector shapes or geometric lines behind the photo container.
- Add floating glassmorphism badges, icons, or star tags.
- Use soft drop shadows or custom border radiuses to match your web components.

## 6. Combine Multiple Assets into a Collage

Instead of relying on one hero image to tell your story, combine 2 or 3 thematic photos into a clean grid or overlapping card collage.

When mixed together with on-brand typography and consistent spacing, stock photos transform from generic placeholders into a compelling visual narrative.
