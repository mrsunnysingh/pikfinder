A clean cut-out makes product shots, profile pictures and logos look professional. You don't need Photoshop or any design skill — you can remove a background online for free in seconds, right in your browser.

## One-click background removal

1. Open the free [background remover](/tools/background-remover).
2. Upload your photo.
3. The subject is detected automatically and the background is removed — download a transparent PNG.

It works best on photos with a clear subject: people, products, pets, and objects that stand out from what's behind them.

## Why you want a transparent PNG

When the background is removed, the image is saved as a PNG with a transparent area where the background used to be. That means you can drop the cut-out onto *any* colour, gradient, or photo and it blends in seamlessly — no white box around it. JPG can't do this (it has no transparency), which is why background removal always exports PNG.

## What to do with the cut-out

- **Products:** drop the subject onto a clean white or branded background for a catalogue or marketplace look that converts better.
- **Profile pictures:** place yourself on a solid brand colour for a consistent, professional team page.
- **Logos & graphics:** get a transparent version that sits nicely on light or dark designs.
- **Stickers & thumbnails:** cut-outs pop on YouTube thumbnails, social posts, and printed stickers.

## Put it on a new background

Once you have a transparent PNG, open [Creator Studio](/studio) to place it on a gradient, a solid colour, or one of thousands of free stock photos — then export in HD. This is how you turn a plain phone photo into a polished product image without a studio.

## Tips for a clean edge

- Use a photo where the subject clearly stands out from the background.
- Good, even lighting gives the cleanest cut — avoid harsh shadows behind the subject.
- For fine details like hair or fur, a higher-resolution photo helps the tool trace the edge.
- If a stray bit of background remains, try a photo with more contrast between subject and backdrop.
