Passport, visa and exam applications all want a photo at an exact size with a plain background. A studio trip costs money and takes time — but you can make a compliant photo from a phone snap in a couple of minutes, for free.

## What a passport photo needs

Most authorities ask for the same basics, though you should always check your country's exact rules:

- **A plain, light background** — usually white or off-white.
- **A straight, front-facing pose** with a neutral expression and both eyes open.
- **Even lighting** with no harsh shadows behind the head.
- **The correct size** — 2×2 inches (51×51 mm) in the US, or 35×45 mm in India, the UK and much of Europe.

## Make one in three steps

1. **Take a clear photo.** Stand against a light wall in soft, even light. Face the camera straight on.
2. **Open the [passport photo maker](/tools/passport-photo-maker).** Upload your picture and pick your country's size — it crops and positions the face for you.
3. **Download.** Save the properly sized photo, ready to upload or print.

## Fix a busy background

If your wall isn't plain, remove the background first with the free [background remover](/tools/background-remover), then drop in a clean white background before sizing. That turns almost any snapshot into a usable passport photo.

## Meeting a file-size limit

Many portals also cap the file size — often 20KB to 100KB. After sizing your photo, run it through the [image compressor](/tools/compress-image) to hit the exact limit without it looking blurry. For very strict forms there are dedicated tools for [20KB](/tools/compress-image-to-20kb) and [50KB](/tools/compress-image-to-50kb).

## Tips for an accepted photo

- Keep your hair off your face and avoid heavy shadows.
- Don't smile widely — a neutral expression is safest.
- Remove glasses if your authority requires it (many now do).
- Print on photo paper if a hard copy is needed.
