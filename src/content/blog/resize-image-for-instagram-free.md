Upload a photo that's the wrong shape and Instagram crops it — often chopping off the important part. Resizing to the right dimensions first keeps your whole image visible and sharp. Here's the free way to do it.

## The Instagram sizes that matter

- **Square post:** 1080 × 1080 px (1:1) — the classic feed post.
- **Portrait post:** 1080 × 1350 px (4:5) — takes up the most feed space.
- **Story / Reel:** 1080 × 1920 px (9:16) — full-screen vertical.
- **Landscape post:** 1080 × 566 px (1.91:1).

Portrait (4:5) is worth knowing — it's the largest a feed photo can be, so it stands out most as people scroll.

## Resize in three steps

1. **Open the [Instagram resizer](/tools/resize-image-for-instagram).**
2. **Upload your photo** and pick the format — post, portrait or story.
3. **Download** the correctly sized image, ready to upload with no in-app cropping.

Prefer to set exact numbers yourself? The general [resize tool](/tools/resize-image) lets you type any width and height.

## Avoid the quality drop

- **Start big.** Resizing a small image *up* makes it blurry. Begin with the largest version you have.
- **Don't over-compress.** If the file gets huge after resizing, use the [image compressor](/tools/compress-image) to slim it down without visible loss.
- **Keep the subject centred** so nothing important sits near an edge that might get cropped in previews.

## Design a post, not just a photo

If you want text, a logo or a branded look, build the whole post in [Creator Studio](/studio) — it has ready Instagram sizes, premium templates and HD export. Great for promos, quotes and announcements.

## Tips

- Export at 1080 px wide; Instagram displays at that width, so bigger just wastes size.
- Use PNG for graphics with text, JPG for photos.
- Batch-resize a set of photos to keep a consistent grid.
